Traffic Light Protocol (TLP) how far an advisory may be shared

Every advisory carries a TLP level, chosen by the advisor: it is printed at the top and bottom of every page of the report and kept in its JSON. It tells the recipients how far they may pass it on. Looking Glass follows TLP 2.0 by FIRST (first.org/tlp); the summaries below are ours.
