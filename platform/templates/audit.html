Integrity and evidence

Each entry of the audit log carries the fingerprint (SHA-256) of the entry before it, so changing, inserting or removing any entry breaks the chain. Check it here, then take the evidence for the period. Every check and export is itself in the audit log.

The proof package lets you check it without trusting Looking Glass: the log's files exactly as stored, the SHA-256 of each, and a short verifier in plain Python (standard library only) that recomputes the whole chain on your own computer. Compare with an earlier audit

Enter the head noted at an earlier audit (hash@entry, as printed in its report or package). Each entry's hash depends on every entry before it: if the hash is still at that entry number, the log up to it is unchanged since then, even if someone could rewrite the whole file.
