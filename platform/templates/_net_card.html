Network names & types

The company name and sector shown for each network (AS number) on both platforms, in analyses, filters and reports. Automatic names (PeeringDB, RIPEstat) are often the legal holder: {% if readonly %}researchers and admins correct them on the Definitions page{% else %}correct them here{% endif %}, for example "Example Holdings Limited" to "Example Telecom". Corrected networks are marked reviewed and never overwritten; every change is in the audit log.

Other address ranges

Ranges or single addresses that belong to no listed network, for example a government network without its own AS number. Ranges not seen in the reports yet are kept here too. Remove one with its × button; Apply settings, at the bottom, saves the change.
