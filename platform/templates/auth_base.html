Looking Glass

Looking Glass turns the daily Shadowserver reports on your constituency into a picture your team can act on: infected devices calling botnet sinkholes and attacking others, replayed on a map, and the services your networks expose to the internet with their known vulnerabilities, region by region.

It is built for CSIRTs and security operations teams, to find the hosts to follow up, analyse what is happening and brief management and partners.
